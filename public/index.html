Cryptographic Lifecycle

How the Vault Works

From browser encryption to physical server deletion — the secret never travels in the open.

Step 01

Create

Draft your confidential note or upload credentials, tokens, or scripts.

Step 02

Encrypt

AES-256-GCM generates a random 96-bit IV and authenticated ciphertext tag in your browser.

Step 03

Share Link

Dispatch the single-use link through WhatsApp, Slack, Teams, or email.

Step 04

Authorize

Backend validates context and issues a single-use reveal token.

Step 05

Reveal & Shred

Decrypted in browser RAM; database row is physically purged immediately after.
