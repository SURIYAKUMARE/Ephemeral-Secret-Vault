IN-BROWSER WORKFLOW

Zero Plaintext in Message Drafts

Experience seamless security without altering your day-to-day communication habits.

01

Write or Paste

Open any chat message in WhatsApp, Gmail, or Slack. Press Ctrl+Shift+V or right-click to trigger the vault modal.

02

Smart Detection

The extension automatically recognizes API tokens, AWS keys, JWTs, and passwords, suggesting instant protection.

03

One-Click Vaulting

The plaintext is immediately encrypted and replaced with a temporary, single-use vault link in the draft field.

04

Recipient Reveals

Your contact clicks the link, views the credentials once on any device, and the vault vanishes permanently.

SECURITY ARCHITECTURE

Engineered for Zero Data Leakage

Manifest V3 compliance ensures minimal browser permissions and strict isolation.

Isolated Shadow DOM

The in-page creation modal is rendered inside a closed Shadow Root, guaranteeing webpage scripts cannot inspect the secret.

Zero Plaintext in Storage

The extension never writes secrets or encryption keys into chrome.storage.local or session buffers. Only anonymous metadata is logged.

Native Platform Adapters

Dedicated adapters hook directly into the contenteditable and draft containers of WhatsApp, Gmail, Outlook, Slack, and Discord.
