What It Does
Extension Features
Direct Message Integration
Inject secure vault links directly into WhatsApp Web, Gmail, Slack, Outlook, Discord, and Teams chat boxes — without leaving the page.
Secure Clipboard Workflow
Copy text from your clipboard, create an encrypted vault, and share the link — all in two clicks. No secrets touch the chat platform.
One-Click Expiry Control
Set expiry time and view limits directly in the popup without leaving your current tab. 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days — your choice.
Zero Plaintext in Chat
The actual secret never appears in the messaging platform. Only the secure vault link is sent — which auto-destructs after reading.
