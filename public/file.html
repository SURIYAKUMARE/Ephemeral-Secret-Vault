OPERATING MODEL

How Ephemeral Secret Vault Works

An end-to-end zero-trace architecture from client transmission to disk zero-overwriting.

01

Payload Submission

You enter credentials or attach confidential files (up to 10 MB) and configure maximum views and time-to-live.

02

AES-256-GCM Encryption

Payload is encrypted with a unique 256-bit key and fresh 12-byte initialization vector. The key never leaves memory unprotected.

03

Zero-Trace Destruction

Once viewed or expired, the record is immediately and permanently deleted using atomic SQLite execution.

SECURITY ARCHITECTURE

Cryptographic Safeguards & Threat Defense

Engineered to eliminate persistent credential exposure in high-compliance environments.

Passphrase Brute-Force Shield

Passphrases salted and hashed with scrypt. Automatically auto-destructs after 3 invalid attempts.

Zero Plaintext At Rest

Only encrypted ciphertext and authentication tags reside in SQLite WAL storage.

Active Background Cleaner

Continuous automated purging runs every 10 seconds to sweep and erase expired secrets immediately.
